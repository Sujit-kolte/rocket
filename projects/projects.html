APJ


The APJ rocket achieved an apogee of 10,000 feet at the Spaceport America Cup 2018, securing 89th place globally in the COTS category.


Built entirely from scratch, it showcased outstanding innovation and performance, inspiring future amateur rocketry advancements.






APJ II


APJ II, designed for the Spaceport America Cup 2019, featured key enhancements like a superior telemetry system and upgraded GPS modules.


Overcoming recovery challenges, it achieved 99% flight accuracy, securing 29th place globally and 1st in the Asia-Pacific region, showcasing the team’s technical growth and resilience.



Perfect Game


In 2021, the team participated in the Latin American Space Challenge (LASC) in São Paulo, Brazil, with "The Perfect Game." Competing in the 0.5 km height target category, the rocket secured 2nd place in India and 6th overall.


The competition marked our first appearance with a team of new recruits after the COVID-19 pandemic, demonstrating our adaptability and continued passion for rocketry.





Midnight Sun


In the 2022 Latin American Space Challenge, our rocket "Midnight Sun" competed in the 1 km solid motor category against teams from nine countries. It was our debut with an SRAD rocket.


Developed entirely in-house with a custom-built solid motor, it secured 3rd in category and 8th worldwide, proving our capabilities in independent propulsion design.





Load Save




The Load Save rocket displayed remarkable reliability and technical performance across several competitions. Its successful launches were a testament to the engineering skills behind it.


It made a significant contribution to the ongoing growth and innovation within amateur rocketry, setting a high standard for future projects.



Stellar Eye



In the 2023 Latin American Space Challenge, our rocket STELLAR EYE secured 1st in Asia, 4th in Target Category, and 10th Globally. Powered by our custom SRM, Lakshya, and supported by the in-house developed “Endeavour” for flight data.


It showcased our advancements in rocketry. Additionally, Project LEVI advanced research on the Doppler effect with an acoustic levitator.



Demogorgon



The Demogorgon rocket made a lasting impression with its consistent performance and reliability in several competitions. Its successful launches highlighted the strength of its engineering design.


It played a crucial role in pushing the boundaries of amateur rocketry, inspiring new innovations and technical excellence.

The trajectory of STES Rocketry

2017

IGNITION

STES Rocketry was founded by a group of passionate engineering students. The dream: to build rockets and represent India on the global stage.

2018

LIFTOFF: APJ

Our first rocket, APJ, achieved an apogee of 10,000 feet at Spaceport America Cup 2018, securing 89th place globally. A humble beginning to a soaring journey.

2019

APJ II: ASCENT

With superior telemetry and GPS, APJ II achieved 99% flight accuracy. We secured 29th place globally and 1st in the Asia-Pacific region at Spaceport America Cup 2019.

2021

PERFECT GAME

LASC Brazil. Competing in the 0.5 km category, we secured 2nd place in India and 6th overall. Precision engineering at its finest.

2022

MIDNIGHT SUN

Our SRAD debut. Developed entirely in-house with a custom solid motor. Secured 3rd in category and 8th worldwide. The fire burns brighter.
